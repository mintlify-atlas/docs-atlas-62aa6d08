VuFind is an open source discovery environment that gives library patrons a single, unified interface to search across your catalog, institutional repositories, and third-party databases. Built on PHP and Apache Solr, VuFind integrates with 30+ integrated library systems and supports multiple external search backends including EDS, Primo, Summon, and WorldCat.

## What VuFind does

VuFind replaces or supplements your library's OPAC with a modern, faceted search experience. Patrons can search across bibliographic records, find holdings information from your ILS, place holds, manage their accounts, and discover related materials — all from one interface.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install VuFind">
    Download the latest release and run `php install.php` to configure your environment. VuFind requires PHP 8.2+ and Apache Solr.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your ILS">
    Edit `config/vufind/config.ini` to point VuFind at your integrated library system. Choose from 30+ supported drivers in the `[Catalog]` section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Index your records">
    Import MARC records using the provided import scripts, or configure OAI-PMH harvesting to keep your index in sync automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and deploy">
    Apply your institution's branding through the theme system, configure facets and search fields to match your collection, then go live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
